🎶 About Chords Music Academy

Welcome to Chords Music Academy, a trusted destination for quality music education. Founded by CN Aditya, Music Composer and Keyboard Programmer in the Telugu film industry, our academy is committed to nurturing musical talent with a perfect balance of tradition and modernity.

🎹 Meet Our Founders

CN Aditya - Founder

Music Director in Telugu Film Industry with 20+ years of experience. Trained under legendary PS Nityanand (A.R. Rahman's mentor). Trinity Grade 8 Piano Certified.

Founded Chords Music Academy in 2018 to combine professional discipline with creative freedom, creating a space where music is taught with both structure and soul.

C.Satya Brahmani - Co-Founder

Singer, Carnatic Vocalist & Piano Trainer with 10+ years in music education. Expert in both traditional Carnatic music and modern teaching methods.

Known for patient approach and personalized attention, helping students discover their musical potential through authentic classical training.
