Piano & Keyboard

From your very first note to performing full songs — our piano curriculum blends Western music theory with Indian and Carnatic styles, taught through a structured level system.

Beginner Friendly Trinity Aligned Online & Offline All Ages
🎼
Trinity Syllabus
We follow the official Trinity College London curriculum — starting with basics like notes, scales, sight reading and ear training. Later grades include music theory, performance pieces, improvisation and exam preparation.
🧑‍🏫
Teaching Approach
  • ▸Step-by-step from fundamentals
  • ▸Proper finger technique
  • ▸Rhythm & timing focus
  • ▸Songs + Trinity pieces together
  • ▸Weekly practice guidance
  • ▸Slow teaching until comfort & confidence
Grade Progression
Initial – Grade 2
Foundation
First 6–12 months · 2 classes/week
  • Hand posture, finger placement & sitting position
  • Notes on keyboard — white & black keys
  • Basic staff notation — treble & bass clef
  • Major scales — C, G, D, F
  • Simple pieces & exercises (Trinity Initial–Grade 1)
  • Sight reading — single line melodies
  • Ear training — recognise simple patterns
  • Rhythm & beat counting with metronome
Grade 3 – Grade 5
Development
1–2 years · 2 classes/week
  • All major & minor scales + arpeggios
  • Trinity Grade 3–5 performance pieces
  • Sight reading — two-hand coordination
  • Music theory — intervals, chords, keys
  • Ear training — melodic & harmonic intervals
  • Dynamics, articulation & expression
  • Indian film songs alongside Trinity pieces
  • Improvisation introduction
Grade 6 & Above
Performance
2+ years · 2 classes/week
  • Advanced Trinity performance repertoire
  • Music theory — modes, harmony, composition
  • Full improvisation & creative musicianship
  • Exam preparation & mock assessments
  • Stage performance & recital readiness
  • Recording session skills
  • Original composition techniques
  • Concert & exam certification

What you'll achieve

Play full songs independently
Read sheet music
Trinity grade certification
Play Indian & Western styles
Perform on stage
Understand music theory

Carnatic Vocals

A traditional and systematic approach to Carnatic music — from basic swaras and varisais to kritis and ragam exploration. Suitable for children and adults with no prior background.

Traditional Method Ragas & Kritis Online & Offline Age 5+
Grade Progression
Initial – Grade 2
Prathamika (Foundation)
First 6–12 months · 2 classes/week
  • Saptaswaras — Sa Ri Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni
  • Sarali varisai (basic note exercises)
  • Janta varisai (double note exercises)
  • Breathing technique & voice placement
  • Introduction to tala — Adi tala (8 beats)
  • Shruthi awareness (Sa-Pa relationship)
  • Basic gamakas (ornaments)
  • Simple devotional & folk songs
Grade 3 – Grade 5
Madhyama (Development)
1–2 years · 2 classes/week
  • Alankarams — all 35 tala variations
  • Geetams & Swarajatis
  • Varnams — detailed study of a raga
  • Basic kritis — Thyagaraja & Muthuswami Dikshitar
  • Introduction to ragas (Mayamalavagowla, Hamsadhwani)
  • Layam — tala practice & konnakkol basics
  • Neraval & sangati variations
  • Ear training — swara recognition & shruthi accuracy
Grade 6 – Grade 8
Uttama (Performance)
2+ years · 2 classes/week
  • Multiple kritis across various ragas
  • Raga alapana — elaboration without rhythm
  • Ragam Tanam Pallavi (RTP) — concert form
  • Manodharma sangeetam — improvisation
  • Advanced gamakas & oscillations
  • Concert format & stage presentation
  • Exam & competition preparation
  • Grade 6–8 certification readiness

What you'll achieve

Sing with correct shruthi
Perform kritis on stage
Identify and sing ragas
Improvise neraval
Grade certification
Concert readiness

Drums

Learn to keep the groove from day one. Our drums curriculum covers rudiments, genre beats, fills and full song playalongs — taught on acoustic and electronic drum kits.

Acoustic & Electronic Rock · Pop · Bollywood Offline Classes Age 7+
Grade Progression
Initial – Grade 2
Foundation
First 6–12 months · 2 classes/week
  • Drum kit anatomy & stick grip
  • Basic rudiments — single stroke, double stroke
  • Kick-snare-hi-hat basic beat (4/4)
  • Quarter note and eighth note patterns
  • Simple rock & pop beats
  • Introduction to drum notation reading
  • Basic fills between phrases
  • Metronome training & tempo awareness
Grade 3 – Grade 5
Development
1–2 years · 2 classes/week
  • Paradiddles & advanced rudiments
  • 16th note hi-hat patterns & ghost notes
  • Different time signatures — 3/4, 6/8
  • Genre beats — blues, funk, hip-hop, Bollywood
  • Crash & ride cymbal usage
  • Complex fills — 4-bar & 8-bar phrases
  • Music theory — notation, dynamics, tempo
  • Trinity Grade 3–5 repertoire & exercises
Grade 6 – Grade 8
Performance
2+ years · 2 classes/week
  • Jazz comping & swing patterns
  • Polyrhythm & odd time signatures (5/4, 7/8)
  • Double bass pedal techniques
  • Full song playalongs (studio recordings)
  • Band coordination & live performance
  • Brush technique for jazz/acoustic sets
  • Trinity Grade 6–8 exam preparation
  • Drum solo composition & stage performance

What you'll achieve

Play full songs end to end
Rock & Bollywood grooves
Trinity grade certification
Perform with a band
Record in a studio
Read drum notation
