User Manual v2 — Chords Music Academy
1. What is SwaraSheet?
SwaraSheet captures notes from your MIDI keyboard or the on-screen piano keyboard and converts them into text notation in real time — Western (C D E F), Indian Sargam (Sa Ri Ga Ma), and Carnatic. Use it to transcribe songs, create note sheets, and export clean PDFs. No internet required once loaded.
2. Connecting Your MIDI Keyboard
Step 1: Connect keyboard via USB cable to your computer.
Step 2: Click the 🔌 Connect button (top right).
Step 3: Allow MIDI access when browser asks.
Step 4: Status dot turns green — you're ready to play.

⚠️ MIDI requires Chrome or Edge browser. Firefox and Safari are not supported.

No MIDI keyboard? Use the built-in 🎹 Keys on-screen piano — click any key to enter a note and hear it play instantly. Shows 3 octaves: C3 (low), C4 (middle), C5 (high). Sound is generated live in the browser — no downloads needed.
3. Notation Formats
Choose your format from the buttons in the Controls section:
LetterC D E F G A B C#
With OctaveC4 D4 E4 F4 (includes octave number)
Indian SargamSa Ri Ga Ma Pa Da Ni
CarnaticS R1 R2 G1 G2 G3 M1 M2 P D1 D2 N3…
DashC-D-E-F (hyphen separated)

Switch format any time — notes automatically re-convert.
4. Octave Indicators (Prime Marks)
SwaraSheet uses prime marks — the traditional Indian music notation standard — to show which octave a note belongs to:
Middle octaveSa   Re   Ga   (no mark)
High octave (Tara)Sa′   Re′   Ga′   (blue ′ above)
Very highSa′′   Re′′   (double prime)
Low octave (Mandra)Sa,   Re,   Ga,   (amber , below)
Very lowSa,,   Re,,   (double comma)

These marks appear in both the app and the PDF export. They are universally used in Indian classical music textbooks.
5. Indian / Carnatic Settings
Sa (Root note): Select which key is Sa — C, C#, D… up to B. All Indian/Carnatic note names shift accordingly.

Carnatic Variants: For ambiguous pitches, choose between:
 • D → R2 or G1    D# → R3 or G2
 • A → D2 or N1    A# → D3 or N2
6. Recording Notes
Play your MIDI keyboard or click the on-screen piano — notes appear in the sequence box instantly.

⏸ Pause / ▶ Resume: Temporarily stop recording so keyboard input is ignored.

Separator: Choose Space, Comma, Dash, or New Line between notes.

Double-click any note to replace it — play a new note from MIDI or on-screen piano to substitute it.
7. Editing Notes
Delete last note: Press Delete / Backspace key, or click ↩ Undo.
Delete any note: Right-click the note → removed instantly.
Redo: Cmd+Shift+Z (Mac) or Ctrl+Y (Windows).
Font size: Use A− / A+ or type a number directly.
New line: Press Enter.
Bar line ( | ): Press Space or click the Bar button.

Transpose: Use ▼−2 ▼−1 ▲+1 ▲+2 buttons to shift all notes up or down by semitones. Chords transpose automatically. Shortcut: Cmd+↑/↓.
8. Section Labels
Click § Label to open the label panel.

Indian: Pallavi, Anupallavi, Charanam, Sthayi, Antara, Sanchari
Western: Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Outro
Repeat markers: ↺ Repeat, ×2, ×3 — appear inline on the same line as notes

Type a custom label and click + Add for anything not listed.
9. Chords
Switch to 🎸 Chord mode and hold keys — the detected chord appears in the "Detected" display.

Click ↑ Apply to attach it to the last note, or click any note's chord label (orange, above the note) to edit or assign a chord directly.

Right-click a chord label to clear it. Chords appear above notes in the sequence and in the PDF.
10. Metronome
Click 🥁 Metro to open the metronome panel.

Set your BPM (40–200) and time signature (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8). Click ▶ Start to begin — the status dot pulses on each beat and accents beat 1. Click ⏹ Stop to end.
11. Copy, Export & Share
📋 Copy All: Copies all notes as plain text — includes chords, octave marks, section labels, bar lines. Paste into WhatsApp, Word, Notes, etc.

📋 per line: Each line has a copy icon at the end — copies just that line.

WhatsApp share: Formats notes with bold/italic markdown for clean WhatsApp display.

Export PDF: Opens a modal — enter song title, Raag, Taal, choose whether to include chords and section labels, then click Export. Saves as a formatted note sheet with academy header and watermark.
12. Saving & Loading Sessions
💾 Save: Type a song title then click Save — stores notes, format, Sa, chords in browser storage. Saving with the same title overwrites the existing entry.

▶ Load: Loads a saved session. Your edits after loading are safe — the saved copy is untouched unless you save again.

+ New Song: Saves current notes to history, then starts a fresh session.

🗑 Clear: Discards all current notes (no history save).

⬇ Download: Saves a .swarasheet file to your computer — use to back up or share.
⬆ Import: Load a .swarasheet file back — works on any device.

Search: Type in the search box to find a saved session by name.

🗑 Bin: Deleted sessions go to Bin first. Click ↩ Restore to recover, or 🗑 to permanently delete.
