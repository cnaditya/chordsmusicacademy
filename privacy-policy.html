Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Aditya's Chords Music Academy ("we", "us") runs music classes in Manikonda, Hyderabad, and online. This page explains what personal information we collect through chordsmusicacademy.in, our registration form and WhatsApp, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • When you register or enquire: the student's name, date of birth, address, phone number and email, a parent or guardian's name for students who are minors, the instrument, level and class mode (online or offline) chosen, and any notes you write to us.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: your phone number and whatever you choose to send us.
  • When you visit our website: basic usage information through advertising and measurement tags (see "Advertising" below).

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and arrange demo or trial classes.
  • To enrol students, schedule classes, keep attendance and manage fees.
  • To understand which of our advertisements lead to enquiries, so we can run them more effectively.

Advertising and measurement

We advertise on Google. Our website uses a Google Ads tag that records when a visitor takes an action such as opening WhatsApp or submitting the registration form. This helps us measure whether our ads work. It is not used to identify you personally by name.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use a small number of service providers that process information on our behalf, such as our website host, the service that emails us new registrations, WhatsApp, and Google for advertising measurement. They may only use the information to provide their service to us.

Students who are minors

Registrations for students under 18 should be made by a parent or guardian. If you believe we hold information about a child that you did not agree to share, contact us and we will remove it.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details for as long as a student is enrolled or an enquiry is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. We take reasonable steps to keep the information secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
